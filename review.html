Method

macOS Screen Recording permission is not granted to Terminal, so screencapture is blocked and I could not photograph windows directly. Instead the real bytes were captured three ways and rendered at 2× retina:

  • script recorded simple one-shot commands through a pty (version, doctor, the not-found error).
  • tmux ran the interactive programs in a real terminal and capture-pane -e exported the rendered screen, colors included. A real terminal did the emulation, so what you see is what was on screen. This is what the installer and every TUI screen used.
  • Live pages were rendered by headless Chrome at --force-device-scale-factor=2.

What was and wasn't touched

First-run screens used throwaway HOME directories — /tmp/cc-tui-home for the TUI screens and /tmp/cc-sandbox4 for the installer — so credentials and the installed binary were never modified. Verified after every step: ~/.local/bin/claude still points at 2.1.231 from Aug 21, and no login was ever completed.

One real change was made, separately and deliberately: a stale Aug 24 update-failure record at ~/.claude/.last-update-result.json was cleared in the real profile, with a backup at .last-update-result.json.bak-20260827. That is why the claude doctor capture reads "none recorded" rather than "failed."
